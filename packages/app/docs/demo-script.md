Target length: **2 minutes 45 seconds**. This is a shotlist, not a completed video. Record the core round on a real Twitch channel after the credentialed acceptance checks. Obtain permission for people and third-party material in the recording.

| Time | Capture | Suggested narration |

## Capture checklist

- Complete the real Twitch, microphone, second-device and OBS checks in [team-and-release-checklist.md](team-and-release-checklist.md) first.
- Keep the core hint, spoken target and reveal in one continuous sequence where practical. Mark time jumps; do not imply that separate recordings were one live exchange.
- Capture genuine material from the main build session with its owner's permission. If only the current improvement chat is available, label it accurately and retain the original session evidence separately. See [build-provenance.md](build-provenance.md).
- Show the submitted version. Do not describe the proposed tabletop modes as implemented.
- Hide credentials, OAuth callback queries, private messages, overlay keys, browser autofill and unrelated account details. Do not open secret files to record setup.
- Use English narration or English captions. Check readable text and speech volume at normal playback speed.
- Keep the final video below three minutes. Upload to YouTube only when publication is authorized, use public or unlisted visibility, and test playback while signed out.
- Put the verified video URL in `devpost-submission.md`. `TODO_YOUTUBE_URL` is not a submission-ready link.

The event requirements request a working Twitch demonstration and visible build work with the coding agent. The event FAQ permits an unlisted YouTube video. This plan becomes evidence only after the footage exists and its links have been checked.
