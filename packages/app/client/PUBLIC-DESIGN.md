Mode: Operate. This extends the landing's selected card (Glass face, Mosaic back), Russo One targets in each item's ink, pixel artwork, the violet ground and small yellow accents. The audience needs to recognize the secret word immediately; the broadcaster needs a readable clock without seeing that word.

The web viewer uses one actual game card, the same Glass card as the Extension overlay, with its clues on the landing's rail and the clock in its footer. The OBS source retains its existing upper-left position for scene compatibility. Its opaque HUD sits on a transparent canvas. On 27 October 2026 the owner asked for its boxes in Twitch's style (`obs-twitch.css`): square and borderless, extruded down and to the left like Twitch's logo, and set in Baited Pixel, an original 5 × 7 pixel font generated by `scripts/build-pixel-font.mjs` into `public/fonts/baited-pixel.ttf`. Its type runs at whole multiples of 8 px (16 px labels, 24 px headlines, a 48 px clock) so the pixels stay crisp at 1920 × 1080. The cards themselves keep the landing's design. Chat's streak is a fire strip in the HUD, and a badge win brings its medallion beside the result card. A new streamer's pick plays a six-second sting in the same make: BAITED BY @name on the landing's table colours, with white and lilac pixel confetti and stepped motion, and no trophy (the design spec dropped trophy artwork). Only the name is shown, never the hint; the corner chip appears when the sting ends. Mission progress is a compact row inside the same frame. Only caught and escaped results get the large reveal below the HUD. Pending, overturned, skipped and ended words are removed at the API boundary and independently guarded in the UI. Paused, reconnecting and winning states have text labels in addition to color.

The rounded card edges match the approved landing rather than the earlier clipped-corner dashboard. Motion is limited to the existing timer and a short result reveal; reduced motion removes animation and confetti. No idle ornament, sound or simulated activity is added.

State coverage by existing markup: waiting, active, paused, final-speech settling, broadcaster catch review, caught, escaped, overturned, mission completion/failure, reconnecting and room error. The stylesheet is scoped to `.viewer-room`, OBS classes and the existing public error view, leaving the privacy article and streamer studio to their own style owners.
