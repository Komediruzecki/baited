The Spicy Mountain

One staged round. No live stream or speech recognition is running on this page.

    Chat knows the word. The streamer doesn't.

    baited is a game for Twitch streams. Every round, chat gets a secret word, and the streamer has no idea what it is.

    Chat has two minutes to get the streamer to say it out loud. If the clock runs out first, the streamer wins the round.

    1. Chat's screen

      Chat gets a secret word

      Viewers see the word and its off-limits clues. The streamer only sees the back of the card.
