# Baited recording: copy your role

Companion to [the shoot guide](2026-10-31-pilot-shoot.md). These are prompts for a **coordinated demonstration on the real game**. Use natural delivery, and keep the actual game result. Today's scored recording is in English because Croatian speech detection has not been verified. Croatian lines below are planning/localization alternatives, not a promise that the English speech model catches them.

## Streamer: Maff or lakeeko

Keep the game, your chat reader and private Studio available. Do not put Studio, its transcript or a secret viewer room in your OBS capture. Tell the team when your microphone and recording are ready.

1. **Banana:** read the clues, say “You are making this one very obvious. I'm calling it,” and type `banana` into Call the bait. Do not say the target before the successful guess. After the win: “Banana. Nice try.” Planned score: 1–0.
2. **Ghost:** talk honestly about horror films. The safe prepared payoff is “That long white mask looks like a ghost.” Say `ghost` as a separate word. “Ghostface” can be transcribed as one token and miss the target. Wait for the actual catch and confirmation. Planned score: 1–1.
3. **Bait:** follow the fish clues. At about ten seconds left, type `fish`; let the real +15 seconds appear. React: “Wrong? Fine. I can survive this.” With about six seconds left, finish “You guys will never bait me.” Pause briefly. Planned final score: 1–2.

Do not force laughter or pretend you had never seen the prepared words. If detection is wrong, use **No, I didn't** during review or **Wrong detection? Reverse win** afterward. If the planned catch does not happen, mark the take and retry rather than fake the scoreboard.

## Paolo: lead baiter

Join the broadcasting channel as yourself, accept the Hosted Test Extension, and check its card before posting. Your screen recording is valuable: it proves the secret word is visible to chat. Do not share that viewer screen with the broadcaster during the natural rounds afterward.

Send one useful message at a time. Leave enough room for the streamer to read and answer it; the table is a menu, not a sequence to paste all at once. Never type the actual target or the card's off-limits clues during an active round. The prepared rounds are easy, but check the card actually shown.

| Round | Start the conversation | Follow-up if needed | After the result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banana | What do you put in a smoothie? | Something yellow. Suspiciously boomerang-shaped. | We were warming up. |
| Ghost | Do you watch horror for the story, or to see who survives? | What's your favorite scary movie? | We only asked about movies. |
| Bait | What swims and ends up on a Friday plate? | Very fishy behaviour from you today. | You said we would never do it. |

For Ghost, “What about the mask in Scream?” is a useful follow-up without typing the target. For Bait, fish is the decoy. One viewer may mention fish if the displayed card permits it; a wall of identical fish messages is unnecessary. Avoid “bait,” “baited” or the actual target's spelling in the live clue thread.

## Second viewer

Build on Paolo's message, rather than sending a competing topic. Use two or three messages over the round, not a burst. Choose the ones you would actually say:

| Ghost follow-up | Which film mask would you recognise immediately? | Koju bi filmsku masku odmah prepoznao? |

Skip any line that contains an off-limits clue on the actual card. Do not paste translated target words into an English scored round and expect them to count as clues or detections correctly.

## Director / recording observer

Before the take, confirm all of these aloud in the private coordination channel:

- Right broadcaster and right viewer account; Extension accepted; secret card visible to viewers.
- Streamer microphone ready; game and chat audible; no stream echo.
- Both recordings running; private windows outside capture.
- Recording take enabled and fresh round-0 score; 120-second round; Auto rounds off.
- One spoken sync marker, then a short gap before Start round.

During the take, note each round's start, target, result and any unusually good reaction. Watch the real score progress: **1–0 → 1–1 → 1–2**. A two-win chat streak only exists if the Ghost and Bait catches actually count consecutively.

At ten seconds left in round three, a private “guess now” cue is enough. Do not paste production instructions into public chat. After the wrong guess, watch the real timer and leave enough time for the complete “bait” sentence and speech finalization. Let the 15-second catch review finish and keep ten seconds of reaction afterward.

If something fails, say “keep recording” first, note what happened, then stop between rounds. Use Restart take only when everyone is ready to repeat from Banana. Restarting the take should not be used to erase an inconvenient result while pretending the same uninterrupted take continues.

## After the planned rounds

Play naturally for at least ten rounds without looking up the future words. Keep the recordings rolling for short reactions between rounds. Useful topics: the most questionable route through the current level, a small game detail you have a strong opinion about, or defending the decision you just made. Personal stories are optional and must be real, comfortable to share and free of other people's private information.
