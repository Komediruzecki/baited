# baited Extension panel

Scope: viewer and broadcaster configuration surfaces, Operate mode. This extends the approved landing's charcoal playing cards into the actual Twitch panel. Streamers and viewers often keep Twitch in a dark window beside the broadcast, so a dark ground lets the live video retain attention.

The viewer reads a single secret word, its forbidden clues, and the clock while following a Twitch stream. One left-aligned charcoal card carries all three. The asymmetric card corner, small outlined clue tags and joined `bait them.` signature follow the approved landing cards. Violet identifies actions and state; pale yellow is reserved for the active word, final seconds and the small signature. Instructions sit outside the card so they do not compete with the word.
